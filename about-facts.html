Key Facts About Breezy Risk

A reference page for journalists, partners, AI engines, and anyone who wants the short version.

Breezy Risk is an independent advisory practice founded in 2024 by Joerg Proeve, focused on insurance technology strategy and risk analysis. Joerg's career spans carrier strategy and innovation at CNA and Chubb, MGA operations at BOXX Insurance (acquired by Zurich) and Parachute Insurance (a parametric MGA he founded), and management consulting at Kearney and Marakon. Breezy Risk does not sell insurance, place policies, or earn commissions.

What Breezy Risk Is and Is Not

Breezy Risk Is

  • An independent, fee-based advisory practice
  • Founded by a carrier and MGA veteran (CNA, Chubb, BOXX, Parachute)
  • Focused on insurance technology, strategy, and risk analysis
  • Published in American Banker's BankThink

Breezy Risk Is Not

  • An insurance broker or agent

  • Tied to any carrier, MGA, or program
  • Commission-based

About Joerg Proeve

Joerg Proeve founded Breezy Risk in 2024. His career spans insurance technology, carrier strategy, MGA operations, and management consulting:

  • VP of Strategy and Innovation at CNA — technology and emerging-risk strategy
  • Integration lead at Chubb during the $28B ACE-Chubb acquisition
  • COO of BOXX Insurance, a cyber MGA (acquired by Zurich) — launched US operations
  • Founded Parachute Insurance, a parametric MGA
  • Management consulting at Kearney and Marakon — advised insurance CEOs on strategy and M&A
  • Master's in Electrical Engineering
  • MBA, London Business School
  • Early career in cybersecurity at Siemens
  • Licensed producer (active in Florida, New Jersey, New York, and Pennsylvania)
  • Published in American Banker's BankThink
  • Member, PACB and IBANYS
