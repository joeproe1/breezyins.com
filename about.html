Who I Am

I'm Joerg Proeve. I've spent my career at the intersection of insurance and technology — leading strategy and innovation at major carriers, building MGAs from scratch, and advising insurance executives on how technology reshapes underwriting, distribution, and risk.

At CNA, I was VP of Strategy and Innovation, leading technology and emerging-risk strategy for one of the largest US commercial carriers. At Chubb, I led integration workstreams across the $28B ACE-Chubb acquisition.

I have also built insurance operations from the other side of the desk. I was COO of BOXX Insurance, a cyber MGA that I helped launch in the US before its acquisition by Zurich. Before that I founded Parachute Insurance, a parametric MGA — designing the product, the platform, and the underwriting model from the ground up.

Earlier in my career I worked in management consulting at Kearney and Marakon, advising insurance CEOs on corporate strategy and M&A.

I hold a Master's in Electrical Engineering and an MBA from London Business School. My first career was in cybersecurity at Siemens — I understand the technology infrastructure that insurance products are built to cover, not just the policy language.

I am a member of PACB and IBANYS.

Published in American Banker's BankThink and Insurance Thought Leadership.

What Breezy Risk Is

Breezy Risk is my independent advisory practice. I founded it in 2024 after seeing the same problems from every seat in the industry: coverage gaps that nobody was surfacing, technology decisions made without underwriting insight, and insurance programs evaluated on price rather than on what they actually cover.

The firm is fee-only. No commissions, no carrier affiliations, no broker of record letter.

The Insights section reflects the range of this work — from line-by-line policy analysis to AI liability, carrier technology strategy, and insurance market structure.
