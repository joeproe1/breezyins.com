Insurance Advisory

I advise carriers, MGAs, and investors on insurance technology strategy and emerging risks, drawing on a career spent building and running insurance operations.

Focus Areas

Carrier Technology Strategy

I help carriers identify where technology investments create underwriting advantage and where they don't. I evaluate product, distribution, and claims technology decisions grounded in carrier operations, not vendor narratives.

MGA Operations

I advise on product design, underwriting operations, and capacity strategies for MGAs and program platforms. From formation through scale, I bring the perspective of building two MGAs from scratch.

AI & Emerging Risks

How AI liability, autonomous systems, and other new technology risks create insurance exposure, and what carriers, MGAs, and insureds need to understand before the market hardens around them.

Background
