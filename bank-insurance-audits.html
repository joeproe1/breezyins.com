Coverage Gaps From Recent Bank Audits

The D&O Policy That Steps Back

Gap: Every D&O I reviewed carries a cyber exclusion. A breach that triggers a regulatory investigation takes the D&O out of the picture. Which wording you have is a question nobody asks until the claim.

Impact: The board's defense falls entirely to the cyber policy, sharing one limit with forensics and notification.

The Vendor Outage Nobody Sized

Gap: Dependent business interruption sublimits ranged from zero to $1M. A multi-day core processor outage would exceed the sublimit before recovery begins.

Impact: Potentially seven figures of uninsured exposure. Nobody had tested the limit against actual vendor dependence.
