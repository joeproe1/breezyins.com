Background

At Chubb, I worked in corporate strategy and then on post-merger integration after a $28B carrier acquisition, building the performance reporting framework across 54 countries. At CNA, I designed the governance framework for the $325M technology portfolio: how projects got prioritized, how the steering committee evaluated trade-offs, and which vendor partnerships the company invested in at scale.

I've also built insurance from the ground up. I co-founded a parametric MGA and served as COO for a cyber MGA, building the platform and scaling distribution across 50 states.

In addition, I support financial institutions with independent insurance audits, reading coverage against operations and examiner expectations.

MBA from London Business School, Master's in Electrical Engineering from Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, started in cybersecurity at Siemens.
